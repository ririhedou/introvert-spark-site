Solo Spark Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Solo Spark helps people discover solo-friendly activities, quiet nearby places, calm ideas, favorites, and gentle inspiration for low-social-energy days.

Information We Collect

Solo Spark does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or profile information.

The app may use your device location only if you choose to allow location access.

Location

Location access is optional. If allowed, Solo Spark uses location to recommend nearby solo-friendly places such as parks, libraries, bookstores, museums, gardens, walking spots, and calm public spaces.

You can use the app without sharing your current location by browsing default ideas or typing a city, neighborhood, or place manually.

Local Storage

Solo Spark stores its curated activity idea library and your saved favorites on your device so the app can work quickly and remember what you saved.

Favorites are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to a Solo Spark server.

Maps and Nearby Search

Nearby recommendations use Apple location and MapKit services. Apple may process map, location, and search requests according to Apple's own privacy practices.

Tracking, Ads, and Analytics

Solo Spark does not currently include third-party advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics SDKs.

In-App Purchases

Optional support purchases are handled through Apple In-App Purchase. Solo Spark does not directly collect or store your payment card information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

Solo Spark is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app changes. If new features such as accounts, analytics, or additional third-party services are added, this policy will be updated to explain those changes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at ririhedou@gmail.com.
