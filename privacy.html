Privacy Policy for Introvert Spark

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Introvert Spark helps people discover solo-friendly activities, calm ideas, nearby quiet places, and gentle inspiration for low-social-energy days.

Information We Collect

Introvert Spark does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or profile information.

The app may use your device location only if you choose to allow location access.

Location

Location access is optional. If allowed, location is used to recommend nearby solo-friendly places such as parks, libraries, bookstores, museums, gardens, and walking spots.

You can also use the app without sharing your current location by browsing default ideas or typing a city, neighborhood, or place manually.

Local Storage

Introvert Spark stores its curated activity idea library on your device so the app can work offline and load ideas quickly.

Tracking, Ads, and Analytics

Introvert Spark does not currently include third-party advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics SDKs.

In-App Purchases

If support or donation features are added, payments will be handled through Apple In-App Purchase. Introvert Spark will not directly collect or store your payment card information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

Introvert Spark is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app changes. If we add new features such as accounts, analytics, dynamic trend imports, or additional third-party services, this policy will be updated.

Contact

If you have questions, contact us at ririhedou@gmail.com.
