Privacy Policy for Introvert Spark Last updated: May 19, 2026 Introvert Spark helps people discover solo-friendly activities, calm ideas, nearby quiet places, and gentle inspiration for low-social-energy days. This privacy policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. Information We Collect Introvert Spark does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or profile information. The app may use your device location only if you choose to allow location access. Location is used to recommend nearby solo-friendly places such as parks, libraries, bookstores, museums, gardens, and walking spots. Location Location access is optional. You can use the app without sharing your current location by browsing the default activity ideas or typing a city, neighborhood, or place manually. If you allow location access, the app uses Apple location and MapKit services to search for nearby places. Apple may process map and search requests according to Apple's own privacy practices. Local Storage Introvert Spark stores its curated activity idea library on your device so the app can work offline and load ideas quickly. This local data is used only for app functionality. Tracking, Ads, and Analytics Introvert Spark does not currently include third-party advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics SDKs. In-App Purchases If support or donation features are added, payments will be handled through Apple In-App Purchase. Introvert Spark will not directly collect or store your payment card information. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Children's Privacy Introvert Spark is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it. Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy as the app changes. If we add new features such as accounts, analytics, dynamic trend imports, or additional third-party services, this policy will be updated to explain those changes.
